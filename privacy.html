Piring.

Privacy policy · Kebijakan privasi

Effective 1 November 2026 (natural voice added the same day, on Gemini's paid tier).

Summary

Piring has no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Your recipes stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your recipes and shopping list is stored in a “Piring” folder in your own Google Drive so your other devices can open it. Nobody else receives your data.

Data stored on your device

Recipes you save or write, favorites, archive, shopping list, cooking sessions, timers and cooking history, and app settings. This data stays in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall Piring.

Google sign-in (optional)

When you sign in with Google, Piring requests only these permissions:

  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in and to let a device switch between family members' accounts.
  • Google Drive, files created by Piring only (drive.file): Piring creates a folder named “Piring” with one file, piring.json, holding your recipes, favorites, archive and shopping list. Piring can open only the files it created itself; it cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.

The sign-in token is kept on your device. Data travels directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any server operated by Piring. Signing out removes your collection from that device; it stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can remove Piring's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its data by deleting the “Piring” folder in your Google Drive.

Piring's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not read by people.

Recipe search

When you search, Piring loads pages from recipe websites (Cookpad Indonesia, Resep Koki) directly from your device, the same way a web browser would. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Microphone and speech

Voice search uses your device's speech recognition service only while you are speaking a search; the microphone is not used otherwise.

Reading steps aloud uses either your device's text-to-speech engine or, when Natural voice is on and the device is online, the Google Gemini API. In that case the text of the cooking step is sent to Google to be turned into speech; nothing else is sent, and the resulting audio is kept on your device so the same step is not sent again. Piring uses Gemini's paid tier, under which Google does not use the submitted text to improve its products (see the Gemini API terms). Natural voice is on by default only on TV. If you would rather not send recipe steps at all, turn Natural voice off in Settings and the device voice is used instead.

Children

Piring is a family app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised on the issues page of this site's repository.
